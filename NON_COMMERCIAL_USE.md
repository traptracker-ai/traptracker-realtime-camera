# Non-commercial use

Trap Tracker AI tools are free to use for non-commercial conservation, biodiversity monitoring, ecological research, academic research and education.

Permitted non-commercial uses include:

- university research projects;
- conservation charity and NGO research where the tool is not being sold as a service;
- student projects and teaching;
- non-commercial biodiversity monitoring;
- evaluation and experimentation with your own compatible models.

You may copy, modify and share the software for these non-commercial purposes, provided that attribution is retained and the Trap Tracker AI licence terms are included.

Model weights are not included in this repository. Official Trap Tracker AI model releases will be distributed separately, normally through Zenodo, with their own model cards and licence terms.
